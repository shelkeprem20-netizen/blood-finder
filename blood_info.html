DONATION PROCESS

What happens when
you donate?

The donation process generally consists of several simple steps.

01

Registration

Provide your basic information and complete the donor registration process.

02

Health Check

Staff check relevant health information and determine whether you can donate.

03

Donation

Blood is collected using sterile equipment by trained healthcare professionals.

04

Recovery

After donation, you can rest and follow the advice provided by the donation staff.

BEFORE YOU DONATE

Eligibility depends
on your health.

Blood donation eligibility can depend on factors such as age, health, medications, recent illnesses, travel and previous donations.

Requirements can vary by blood donation service. Always follow the assessment and advice of qualified healthcare professionals.

✓
Be in suitable health Follow the donation centre's health requirements.
✓
Complete the health screening Answer health questions honestly.
✓
Follow professional advice The donation centre makes the final eligibility decision.
✓
Keep your information accurate Accurate donor information helps maintain safer records.
COMMON QUESTIONS

Blood donation basics.

How often can I donate blood?

The interval depends on the type of donation, your health and the rules followed by the donation service. Follow the interval given by the healthcare professional.

Can everyone donate blood?

No. Donors must meet the eligibility criteria of the blood donation service. A health screening is performed before donation.

Why is blood donation important?

Blood and blood components are needed for many medical treatments, surgeries and emergencies.

What should I do before donating?

Follow the instructions provided by your donation centre and provide accurate health information during screening.
