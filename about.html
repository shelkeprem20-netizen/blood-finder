ABOUT BLOOD FINDER

Technology with a
human purpose.

Blood Finder is a college project designed to make connecting blood donors with people who need blood simpler, faster and more organized.

OUR PROJECT

Making blood
discovery simpler.

Finding a suitable blood donor during an emergency can be difficult. Blood Finder provides a simple platform where donors can register their information and people can search for available verified donors.

The system organizes donor information using blood group, location, availability and verification status. This helps make the search process more structured.

Blood Finder is developed as an educational college project to demonstrate how web technologies and databases can be used to address a real-world problem.

OUR MISSION

Connect the right people when every moment matters.

Our goal is to create a simple digital platform that helps organize blood donor information and makes it easier to search for suitable donors.

❤️
HOW BLOOD FINDER WORKS

From registration
to connection.

STEP 01
🩸

Donor Registration

A donor provides basic information such as name, phone number, blood group, location, last donation date and availability.

STEP 02
🔍

Search

A requester can search for donors using the required blood group and location.

STEP 03
🤝

Connect

Suitable verified and available donor information can then be used to contact the donor.
